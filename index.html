What is First Aid?

First aid is the immediate help given to an injured or ill person before a doctor or ambulance arrives. Quick and correct action can reduce pain, prevent the injury from getting worse, and sometimes save a life. This website explains simple first aid steps for common emergencies.

The 3 golden rules

  1. Stay calm and check that the area is safe.
  2. Call for emergency help (112) if the situation is serious.
  3. Give first aid only as far as you know how to.

Aims of First Aid

  • Preserve life
  • Prevent the condition from getting worse
  • Promote recovery

NOTE: This website is for awareness only. It does not replace training or a doctor's advice.

CPR

For an unresponsive person who is not breathing normally:

  1. Call 112 or ask someone else to call.
  2. Place the heel of your hand on the centre of the chest.
  3. Push hard and fast, about 5-6 cm deep, at 100-120 pushes per minute.
  4. Let the chest rise fully between pushes.
  5. Continue until help arrives.

Learn CPR properly from a certified first aid course.

What to tell the emergency operator

  • Your exact location
  • What happened
  • Number of people injured
  • Your phone number

Remember

Stay calm, act quickly, and always call for help in serious emergencies.

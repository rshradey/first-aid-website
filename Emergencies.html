Common Emergencies

Step-by-step first aid for everyday situations

Jump to an emergency

Burns

  1. Cool the burn under running water for 10-20 minutes.
  2. Remove rings or tight items before swelling starts.
  3. Cover loosely with a clean, non-stick dressing.
  4. Do not burst blisters
  5. Do not apply ice, butter or toothpaste.

Get medical help if the burn is large, deep, or on the face or hands

Bleeding (cuts and wounds)

  1. Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth.
  2. Do not remove the cloth if it soaks through. Add more layers on top.
  3. Raise the injured limb if possible.
  4. Seek medical help for heavy bleeding.

Nosebleed

  1. Sit down and lean slightly forward.
  2. Pinch the soft part of the nose for about 10 minutes
  3. Do not tilt the head back.
Back to top

Choking (adult)

  1. Encourage the person to cough.
  2. If they cannot cough or speak, give 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades
  3. Then give 5 abdominal thrusts.
  4. Alternate both until the object comes out, and call 112

Fainting

  1. Lay the person flat and raise their legs.
  2. Loosen tight clothing and give fresh air.
  3. Call for help if they do not recover within a minute or two.
Back to top

Sprain

Use the RICE method:

  • Rest the injured part
  • Ice (wrapped in cloth) for 15-20 minutes
  • Compression with a bandage
  • Elevation above heart level
Back to top

Fracture (suspected broken bone)

  1. Do not move the injured part or try to straighten it.
  2. Support it in the position found.
  3. Call for medical help.
Back to top

Electric shock

  1. Switch off the power first. Never touch the person while they are still in contact with the current
  2. If you can't switch it off, push the person away using a dry wooden stick.
  3. Call 112 and check their breathing.
Back to top

Snake bite

  1. Keep the person calm and still.
  2. Keep the bitten limb still and remove rings or tight items.
  3. Take them to a hospital immediately.
  4. Do not cut the wound, suck the venom or tie a tight band.
